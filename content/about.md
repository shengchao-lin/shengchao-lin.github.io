I got my Ph.D. in Computational and Applied Mathematics at [Rice University](https://cmor.rice.edu/). My research lies mainly in parallel methods for PDE-constrained optimization and recurrent neural networks. I was also co-organizer of an applied math reading group.

I am a Senior Software Engineer on the Optimization Toolbox team at [MathWorks](https://www.mathworks.com/products/optimization.html). My recent work includes modular architecture for ADMM-based algorithms, C/C++ code generation for optimization solvers, and algorithm enhancements for linear, quadratic, conic, and nonlinear optimization.

During my personal time, I enjoy cooking and photography. The photos used on this website are my own, except for the avatar.
