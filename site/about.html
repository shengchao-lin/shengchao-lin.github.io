About

Background, current work, and interests.

I got my Ph.D. in Computational and Applied Mathematics at Rice University. My research lies mainly in parallel methods for PDE-constrained optimization and recurrent neural networks. I was also co-organizer of a reading group.

I am the lead developer in embedded optimization at the Optimization Toolbox team at MathWorks. My work focuses on general nonlinear optimization algorithms and their deployment in safety-critical areas.

During my personal time, I enjoy cooking and photography. The photos used on this website are my own, except for the avatar.
